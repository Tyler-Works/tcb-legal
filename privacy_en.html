THE CLEAN BREAKERS Playtest — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 / Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TylerWorks (the "Provider") handles information in the Playtest version of the game "THE CLEAN BREAKERS" (the "Playtest").

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Provider: TylerWorks
Contact: contact form https://forms.gle/71q32SF4WA52dhFV9

2. Consent and when the Playtest connects to online services

  1. When you launch the Playtest for the first time, a consent screen for the Terms of Use and this Policy is shown. Until you agree, the Playtest does not connect (sign in) to Unity's online services.
  2. After you agree, each time you launch the Playtest it automatically signs in anonymously to Unity's service (Unity Authentication). This also applies when you only play "Bot Match" alone.
  3. The versions of the Terms and Policy you agreed to are saved on your PC. If the Terms or this Policy change materially, the consent screen is shown again.
  4. After you agree, each time you launch the Playtest it reads one small file placed on GitHub, to check for announcements and versions (see "Startup check" in section 3). It does not read it until you agree.

4. Sharing and service providers

  1. We do not share your information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
  2. We entrust the following processing to service providers:
    • Unity Technologies (Unity Authentication, Unity Relay): anonymous sign-in, creating/joining rooms, relaying traffic (Unity's Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
    • Google LLC (Google Forms): receiving and storing inquiries and bug reports
  3. The following companies handle information under their own policies:
    • Valve Corporation (Steam): Playtest sign-up and distribution
    • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages and Gist): hosting this page and serving the file the Playtest reads at startup. GitHub may log the IP addresses of page visitors and connecting devices for security purposes
  4. Players in the same room (including the host) can see your player name and chat.

6. Retention

  • Data on your PC (name, settings, career stats, logs, match records, consent record) may remain after you uninstall the game. See section 8 to delete it.
  • Logs, match records, and inquiries received by the Provider are kept until the official release of the game and then deleted. If we decide not to release the game, they are deleted at that time.
  • Retention by Unity, Google, Valve, and GitHub follows their own policies.

7. Security

Network traffic uses Unity Relay's encryption (DTLS). The Provider stores logs and other submissions in places where access is limited to the Provider. However, no system is completely secure.

8. Your rights and deleting data

  • You can delete data on your PC yourself:
    • Name, settings, consent record: the registry key HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\TylerWorks\THE CLEAN BREAKERS
    • Career stats, logs, match records: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\TylerWorks\THE CLEAN BREAKERS\
  • To request disclosure, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of information the Provider holds (such as submitted logs or inquiries), contact us through the form in section 1. We will verify your identity and respond in accordance with the law.
  • For information held by Unity, Google, Valve, or GitHub, please contact them directly. Note that the Provider may not be able to identify or delete your Unity anonymous authentication ID.
  • The Provider does not sell personal information.

9. Withdrawing consent

To withdraw your consent, stop using the Playtest and delete the data on your PC as described in section 8 (this also deletes the consent record). To have information held by the Provider deleted, contact us through the form in section 1.

10. Children

The Playtest is intended for users aged 13 or older; users under 13 may not use it. Minors must have their parent's or guardian's consent. If we learn that we have received information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents should make sure their children do not enter personal information as player names or in chat.

11. Changes

We will update this Policy when features such as voice chat, Steam ID usage, automatic submission, or cloud saving are added. For material changes, we will ask for your consent again on the in-game consent screen. Minor changes that do not disadvantage users, such as fixing typos or updating contact details, are made by updating this page.

12. For users in the EU/EEA and the UK

This section applies if the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the UK GDPR applies to our processing of your personal data.

  1. Controller: The Provider (see section 1) is the controller. Unity Technologies processes personal data on our behalf as a processor. Google LLC receives and stores the inquiries you send through the contact form (section 4.2).
  2. Legal bases:
    • Anonymous sign-in, creating and joining rooms, relaying traffic, and the startup check: necessary to provide the Playtest under the Terms of Use (performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
    • Investigating bugs, keeping the Playtest secure and fair, and handling logs, match records, and inquiries that you choose to send us: our legitimate interests in testing and improving the game and responding to you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
    • Online play is not possible without the anonymous sign-in. Sending logs, match records, or inquiries is optional.
  3. International transfers: The Provider is located in Japan, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Unity and Google are located in the United States. Where personal data is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the safeguards offered by those companies, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in Unity's Data Processing Addendum.
  4. Your rights: You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us through the form in section 1. For data on your PC, see section 8.
  5. Complaints: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU/EEA member state where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
